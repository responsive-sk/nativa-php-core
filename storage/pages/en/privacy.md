This site collects as little data as possible. Nothing you send is sold or
used for advertising. What follows is the list of what is actually stored and
the reason it is there.

## The contact form

The form on the [contact page](/contact) sends a name, an address, a topic and
the text of a message by e-mail. Along with the message it stores the IP
address, which exists only to defend against spam.

We use the data only to answer your message. We keep it for as long as the
reply is needed as a record of the correspondence, and we delete it on request.

## Questions below a page

A question below an article or a page stores the published name and the text of
the question. No e-mail address is stored with a question. A question appears
only after it is approved.

## Cookies and local storage

The site uses one technical session cookie. It is created only when you sign in
to the administration or send a form that needs protection against request
forgery. Without it, neither the sign-in nor the form submission could be
secured.

There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies here. The site loads no
scripts, fonts or images from other domains, so a visit does not travel to any
other system.

## Who receives the data

The data is processed by the operator of this site and by the hosting provider
the site runs on. It is not handed to anyone else, except where the law
requires it.

## Your rights

You have the right to know what data we hold about you, to have it corrected or
deleted, to object to its processing and to ask for it to be transferred. To
exercise any of these rights, write to **ahoj@responsive.sk**. We reply and
adjust or delete the data.

If you need the operator's identification details for a contract or an
authority, write to the same address and we will send them.
