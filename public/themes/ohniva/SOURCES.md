## Treatment

`logo.gif` is a GIF transcode of the 125 x 125 RGBA PNG with a binary
transparency index; IE6 draws its background without a filter because the GIF
carries one transparent colour. `logobs.png` is the template's 100 x 100 mark
for the narrow stage and is shipped unchanged. `pattern.gif` and the two panel
textures are the original bytes.

Each thumbnail is the site's own 140 x 105 cache image, not a resize of the
slide. The mapping was confirmed by comparing every thumbnail against every
slide's own resize with `compare -metric RMSE`, and each thumbnail matches the
slide of the same name. The eight slides are the original 600 x 400 JPEGs.

`fire.jpg` from the first cut is gone: `slides/01-ohen.jpg` is the same
photograph at the template's own size, so the theme names one image per slide
and none twice.
